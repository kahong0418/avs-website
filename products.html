LED Forecourt Displays & Lighting

The full AVS product range — Price Change Units, LED tubes, modules, lens strips, edge lighting and POS/IFSF integration, engineered in Malaysia for petrol-station forecourts. Backed by a 3-year standard warranty (extendable to 5) and over 9 years running on-site at Petronas and BHP stations.

The AVS Range

Built for the forecourt, proven outdoors

For Over 9 Years AVS has designed and manufactured LED price displays, tubes, modules and signage for petrol stations across Malaysia and the region. Every product is built around premium LEDs from Cree and Osram and engineered for the realities of an exposed forecourt — sun, rain, heat and vibration — with conformal-coated PCBs, an operating range of −30 to +60 °C, IP54 to IP67 sealing and a 48-hour burn-in test on every unit before it ships.

Why AVS LED

Engineered to keep working outdoors

Forecourt displays run 24/7 in full sun, monsoon rain and pump-island vibration. Here is what makes AVS products hold up.

Premium LEDs

Cree and Osram LEDs only — chosen for consistent colour, brightness and long service life across thousands of hours of operation.

IP54 / IP67 sealing

Sealed casings and IP68 cabling connectors keep out dust and water, with UV-resistant silicone plotting and HDPE or aluminium housings for the elements.

High sunlight readability

High-brightness digits with a 120° viewing angle and 64-level automatic photocell brightness stay crisp in direct sun and dim down cleanly after dark.

Tested before shipping

Every unit clears a 48-hour burn-in, a cold-chamber dry-joint test and vibration testing on conformal-coated PCBs before it leaves the factory.
