Decorative / Branding Lighting Solution

Architectural lines that look good day & night

ColorLine Tube is a decorative linear lighting solution designed to enhance petrol station identity and architectural appearance. It creates a clean, continuous light effect for fascia lines, building edges, and feature outlines — improving visibility and branding presence during both daytime and nighttime.

Product Features

Built for station identity and architectural appearance

  • IP65 protection against dust and water ingress for outdoor use
  • 24V DC low-voltage system for stable, safe operation
  • Cuttable at 22mm / 44mm intervals for a precise, seamless fit to site dimensions
  • Continuous light effect for fascia lines, building edges and feature outlines
  • Shown in red, amber, blue and orange on AVS station installations

Installation Flexibility

Cut to fit — 22 mm / 44 mm intervals

The cuttable 22mm / 44mm interval design provides installation flexibility for different site layouts, allowing the lighting line to follow actual architectural dimensions while maintaining a seamless appearance.
