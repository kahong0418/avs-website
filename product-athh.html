ATHH Green Tube

ATHH Green Tube is a high-brightness linear lighting solution designed for petrol stations, canopy edges, signage, and industrial applications where strong visibility and durability are required. It delivers a consistent green lighting effect for architectural highlighting while maintaining reliable outdoor performance. Built with IP65 dustproof and water-resistant protection, the system operates on a 48V DC low-voltage platform and is engineered for long service life in demanding environments.

Built for outdoor performance

Key Features

ATHH is designed for high-performance outdoor lighting with emphasis on brightness, durability, and installation flexibility. The IP65 rating ensures protection against dust and water ingress, while the 48V DC system provides stable and safe operation. The tube is cuttable at 30mm intervals, allowing precise site adaptation without uneven lighting or waste sections.

  • IP65 protection against dust and water ingress
  • 48V DC system for stable and safe operation
  • Cuttable at 30mm intervals for precise site adaptation
