Distributors & OEM

Partner with a factory-direct LED forecourt manufacturer — products engineered in Malaysia for over 9 years, built for sign makers, integrators and regional distributors.

Partner with AVS

Source LED forecourt products direct from the factory

AVS Display Sdn Bhd manufactures LED Price Change Units (PCU), LED tubes, LED modules and outdoor signage in-house at our own facility in Malaysia — using premium Cree & Osram (US/German) LEDs, automated SMT and wave-soldering lines, and a 48-hour burn-in, cold-chamber and vibration test regime on every build.

That means partners buy at factory-direct terms, not through a reseller chain. We work with sign manufacturers, petroleum-equipment and fuel-brand suppliers, system integrators and regional distributors who need a dependable upstream source of certified forecourt LED hardware — with the drawings, documentation and technical support to win and deliver projects.

The AVS advantage

Why partner with AVS

A manufacturing partner built for forecourt LED — from pricing and customisation through to certification and on-the-ground project support.

Factory-direct pricing & supply

Buy straight from the source. PCUs, ATHH Green and ColorLine tubes, Hyperlite modules and Osram lens strip ship from our own production lines — no middle layer, consistent lead times and stable supply.

Customisable & OEM-ready

Specify digit sizes from 120 mm to 600 mm, Red / Green / White / Dual-Colour, Multi-Segment or 7-Segment fonts, IP54 to IP67 builds, and aluminium-extrusion or HDPE casing — configured to your project or brand.

Full project support

Drawings, datasheets and installation documentation, plus responsive technical support on controllers, IP-rated cabling and POS / IFSF integration — so your engineers can quote and commission with confidence.

Certified products

Hardware certified to IFSF forecourt protocol, CE / EMC (EN 55032 / 55024 / 61000), FCC Part 15 and RoHS (IEC 62321) — documentation your customers and authorities expect.

Proven across forecourt brands

AVS PCUs and signage are installed on monoliths for Petronas, Shell, Petron, Vivo, BHP, Aramco and Uni-Oil — a track record you can stand behind when presenting to fuel-brand customers.

Regional reach

From our Malaysian base we support partners across Southeast Asia and beyond — including dual-colour PCUs that auto-switch red by day to white by night, deployed at Vivo stations in Indonesia.

Who we work with

Built for partners across the forecourt supply chain

If you specify, install or resell forecourt and signage hardware, AVS can sit upstream as your certified LED manufacturing partner.

  • Sign manufacturers — source LED PCUs, tubes, modules, lens strip and edge lighting to build into monoliths, pylons and light boxes.
  • Forecourt & petroleum-equipment suppliers — add certified LED price displays and canopy lighting to your fuel-brand offering.
  • System integrators — link PCUs to forecourt controllers and POS over RS232–RS485 using the IFSF protocol for automatic price updates.
  • Regional distributors — carry a dependable, factory-direct AVS range with the documentation and support to serve your local market.
