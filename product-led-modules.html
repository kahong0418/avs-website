Hyperlite

Signage face illumination / high brightness lighting solution

Hyperlite is a modular LED lighting system designed for signage face illumination. Featuring high brightness output, customisable length, adjustable LED pitch, and aluminium housing for excellent heat dissipation, it delivers reliable and uniform illumination for outdoor signage applications.

Key Features

  • High brightness output
  • Custom length available
  • Adjustable LED pitch
  • Aluminium housing for heat dissipation
  • UV-resistant cover
  • IP65 outdoor protection
  • Field-cuttable (White: every 3 LEDs / Red: every 4 LEDs)

Red module vs white module

Same housing, two LED builds. The white module is brighter and cuts every 3 LEDs; the red module runs cooler and cuts every 4.

Installation Flexibility

Hyperlite supports customisable lengths and adjustable LED pitch, allowing installers to optimise brightness distribution for various signage sizes and layouts. The field-cuttable design enables quick on-site adjustment without compromising performance.

Application

Ideal for petrol station signage, illuminated signboards, lightboxes, channel letters, and other outdoor display applications requiring high brightness and uniform illumination.
