PCU Controllers & Price Sign Terminals

The control hardware behind every AVS Price Change Unit — the PCU Controller mounted inside the monolith, and the Price Sign Terminal that lets your shop staff key and send fuel prices.

The control layer

The brains behind the price board

An AVS Price Change Unit shows the price — but it doesn't decide it. That job belongs to two purpose-built control units that work as a pair on every forecourt installation.

The PCU Controller lives inside the monolith or pylon, distributing data and power to the LED digit panels and managing brightness and back-lighting. The Price Sign Terminal sits on the cashier counter in the shop, giving staff a simple back-lit display and keypad to enter prices and push them to the board.

Together they turn a price change from a ladder-and-clip-numbers chore into a few keystrokes — and they're the same control layer that connects to your POS / IFSF forecourt controller when you want pump and pole prices to stay in sync automatically.

  • One controller drives up to 8 PCUs across a single monolith
  • Wired or 2.4 GHz wireless link from terminal to controller
  • Add-on POS card for IFSF Price Pole / forecourt-controller integration

The workflow

How they work together

From keystroke to lit digit — three steps, no ladder, no climbing the totem.

1 — Key it in

Staff enter the new fuel price on the Price Sign Terminal's 16-button keypad, reading every grade's current price off the back-lit LCD.

2 — Send it

The terminal pushes the change to the controller over a wired link or a 2.4 GHz radio modem — your choice at install, no shop wiring required for the wireless option.

3 — Drive the board

The PCU Controller updates the price across up to 8 PCUs on the monolith, auto-managing brightness and back-light. Add-on IFSF integration is available for sites that want price changes to flow automatically from the POS.
