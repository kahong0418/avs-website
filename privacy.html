Privacy Policy

What personal data avs.my collects, why we collect it, who processes it for us, and how you can reach us about it.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how AVS Display Sdn Bhd ("AVS", "we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website, avs.my, in line with the Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Who we are

AVS Display Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian company that designs and manufactures LED price displays and signage for petrol stations. Our operations are based in Nilai, Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia. Our registered address is listed on our Support page.

For any question about this policy or your personal data, email info@avs.my.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Request a Quote form: your name, company, country, email address, phone number, the product you are interested in, the number of sites and units, your timeline, and any project details you type in.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number, subject and message.
  • Fields marked * are required so we can reply. Other fields are optional. Without the required fields we cannot respond to your form.
  • Messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, Zalo or LINE, including the phone number or account name those apps show us.

Information collected automatically

  • Website analytics: pages viewed, links clicked (WhatsApp, email, phone, Zalo or LINE links), approximate location, device and browser type, collected through Google Analytics using cookies.
  • Advertising measurement: when you submit a form, a one-way encrypted (hashed) copy of the email address and phone number you entered is sent to Google Analytics, which is linked to our Google Ads account, so Google can tell us whether our advertising led to an enquiry. Google receives the hashed value, not the plain text.
  • Meta Pixel: on our Vietnamese-language pages only, the Meta (Facebook) Pixel records page views and taps on Zalo or LINE links, so we can measure our Meta advertising. It does not receive the contents of any form.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and follow up on your project.
  • To provide after-sales service, warranty support and spare parts.
  • To understand which pages and adverts are useful, and improve our website and advertising.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who processes it for us

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside Malaysia:

  • Formspree receives the quote and contact forms and forwards them to our email.
  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads) for analytics and advertising measurement. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • Meta (Meta Pixel) on our Vietnamese-language pages.
  • WhatsApp, Zalo and LINE when you choose to message us through those apps.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also share your enquiry with an authorised AVS distributor or installer in your country when that is needed to serve your project, or when the law requires it.

5. Cookies

Google Analytics and, on Vietnamese-language pages, the Meta Pixel use cookies or similar technology. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website still works without them, but we will not be able to measure your visit.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, then delete them or make them anonymous.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to limit how we use your data;
  • withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop using your data for marketing.

Email info@avs.my with your request. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on it.

8. Security

The website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to enquiry records is limited to AVS staff who need it. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so please do not send passwords or payment card details through our forms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
