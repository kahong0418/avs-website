Signage Backlight / High Evenness Lighting Solution

Osram 384 / 450 Lens Strip

The Osram 384 and 450 Lens Strip is a high-performance signage backlighting solution designed to deliver strong brightness with excellent light uniformity. The integrated optical lens system distributes light evenly across signage surfaces, making it suitable even for shallow lightbox designs.

Built for modular installation, the strip is cuttable every 3 LEDs, allowing flexible site adaptation. The system is sealed with IP65 protection and UV-resistant epoxy to ensure long-term outdoor durability and stable optical performance.

Key Features

Precision optics, modular build, sealed for outdoors

Designed to solve uneven backlighting issues in signage applications, the lens strip improves light distribution through precision optics. The modular structure ensures easy installation and customization, while the IP65 sealing protects against dust and water ingress. UV-resistant epoxy enhances durability under long-term outdoor exposure.

Installation Flexibility

Cut every 3 LEDs to fit any lightbox

The strip is designed for modular signage fabrication, allowing installers to cut every 3 LEDs for precise fitting inside different lightbox sizes. Lens control ensures even illumination even in shallow depth signage, reducing hotspots and dark areas.

Application

Where the lens strip fits

Ideal for illuminated signage, shopfront lightboxes, branding panels, and advertising displays requiring uniform backlighting performance with high visual clarity.

  • Shopfront lightboxes
