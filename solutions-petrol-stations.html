Petrol Station Solutions

End-to-end forecourt LED — fuel-price displays, monolith backlighting, identity lighting and POS price sync — engineered in Malaysia and proven on-site at Petronas and BHP stations for over 9 years.

Built for the forecourt

The forecourt is the hardest place to put a display

A petrol-station price sign has to be legible under direct equatorial sun, survive monsoon downpours and decades of canopy vibration, and never show the wrong number to a passing motorist. Glare washes out weak displays, sealed enclosures fail in the heat, manual price changes are slow and error-prone, and a single dead digit becomes a maintenance call on a 24-hour site.

AVS answers every one of these. Our LED Price Change Units run premium Cree and Osram LEDs with 120° viewing and 64-level automatic photocell brightness, so digits stay crisp at noon and comfortable after dark. IP54-to-IP67 enclosures, IP68 cabling connectors and conformal-coated PCBs are rated −30 to +60 °C, and every board passes a 48-hour burn-in, cold-chamber dry-joint and vibration test before it leaves the line. Prices update wirelessly by remote, terminal, or straight from your POS over IFSF — no ladders, no transcription mistakes.

What we deliver

A complete forecourt LED package, from one manufacturer

Every part of the price pole and station identity — displays, backlighting, lighting and control — designed to work together and built in-house.

LED Price Change Units

Wireless LED fuel-price displays for monoliths and pylons. Multi-Segment and 7-Segment fonts, digit sizes from 120 mm to 600 mm, 120° viewing and Cree/Osram LEDs. View the PCU

Monolith backlighting

Even, energy-efficient illumination for monolith faces and light boxes using Hyperlite LED modules and Osram wide-angle lens strip — bright, uniform, fewer LEDs per area. LED modules

POS price sync

Link your PCUs to the forecourt controller or POS — including IFSF-compatible systems — over RS232–RS485, so pump and pole prices update together automatically, from the shop, with no manual re-keying. How integration works

Identity & decorative lighting

Define the canopy, fascia and building edges with ColorLine and ATHH Green LED tubes — UV-resistant, IP65, cut-to-fit and rated for ≥50,000–80,000 hours of outdoor service. LED tubes

Auto day/night brightness

A built-in photocell drives 64-level automatic dimming, so digits are punchy against midday glare and never glaring at night — protecting legibility and LED lifespan around the clock.

Dual-colour day/night displays

Red/Green/White and true Dual-Colour options — for example red by day switching automatically to white at night, as deployed on Vivo forecourts — for maximum contrast in every light condition.

Why it lasts

Engineered to survive the forecourt for years

Every AVS display is tested and built for outdoor abuse — not adapted from indoor signage.

  • 48-hour burn-in test on every unit to catch early-life LED and driver failures before shipping.
  • Cold-chamber (freezer) dry-joint test to expose weak solder joints under thermal stress.
  • Dedicated vibration test for canopy-mounted and roadside installations.
  • Conformal-coated PCBs to resist humidity, condensation and salt-laden coastal air.
  • Sealed enclosures from IP54 up to IP67, with IP68-rated cabling connectors.
  • Wide operating range of −30 to +60 °C and UV-resistant black silicone potting and HDPE casing.
  • Premium Cree and Osram LEDs with a maximum of two LEDs in series for reliable, even output.
