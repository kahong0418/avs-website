Why It Holds Up

The engineering behind every installation

The brands above choose AVS because the hardware survives the Malaysian forecourt — heat, monsoon and vibration — for years.

Premium LEDs only

Every project is built with Cree and Osram LEDs — the same diodes whether it is a single PCU digit or a multi-station rollout.

Tested before it ships

48-hour burn-in, cold-chamber dry-joint testing and vibration testing on conformal-coated PCBs catch failures in the factory, not on site.

Built to be controlled

Remote, terminal or full POS / controller integration keeps pole prices in sync with the pump — one controller managing up to eight PCUs.

Accountability goes both ways: when a board-set needed replacing outside of warranty, AVS covered the full cost of that replacement, a job worth roughly RM100,000, fully self-funded.
