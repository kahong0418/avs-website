LED Solutions, Built Around How You Use Them

AVS engineers and manufactures LED displays and lighting in Malaysia, purpose-built for petrol-station forecourts — start with how AVS serves your industry, or jump straight to the job you need done.

Overview

One LED partner, every petrol-station use case

For over 9 years AVS has designed, manufactured and certified LED hardware for petrol-station forecourts. We build with premium Cree & Osram (US/German) LEDs, test every unit through a 48-hour burn-in, cold-chamber dry-joint and vibration regime, and back the range with IFSF, CE, RoHS and FCC certification. The result is signage and lighting that stays legible and reliable in Malaysia's heat, rain and roadside vibration. Use the two paths below to find the right fit — by your industry, or by the specific display or lighting job you need to solve.

Find the right product for the job

Fuel-price displays (PCU)

Keep every site in your network showing the same accurate price at the same time — updated centrally by remote, terminal or POS/IFSF sync, so no driver pulls up to a stale number and no attendant climbs up to change a sign by hand. Wireless LED Price Change Units for monoliths and pylons: digit sizes from 120 mm to 600 mm, Red/Green/White/Dual-Colour, IP54–IP67 weather sealing, 120° viewing and 64-level auto brightness for legibility from sunrise to night.

Identity & decorative lighting (tubes)

Give every station the same lit-up brand look after dark, in fittings built to keep running through Malaysia's heat, rain and roadside vibration without an early replacement. ATHH Green high-brightness tubes (IP65, rated ≥80,000 h) outline canopies and edges; ColorLine decorative tubes (UV-resistant PMMA diffuser, rated ≥50,000 h) light up fascia and identity — supplied as cut-to-fit modular systems.
