Wireless Remote Control

The simplest, most economical way to update an AVS LED Price Change Unit — a 5-button handheld that changes pole prices from up to 100 metres away.

AVS PCU Wireless Remote

Update prices in seconds — no laptop, no software

The AVS PCU Wireless Remote is the most direct control method for an AVS Price Change Unit. Stand in the forecourt, point the handheld at the monolith, and key in the new fuel price with five simple buttons. The change is sent over radio and reflected on the LED digits straight away.

It is purpose-built for standalone stations and single-monolith sites that want a low-cost, reliable way to manage daily price changes without integrating to a POS or running cabling to the shop. There is nothing to install and nothing to maintain — it runs on a single AA battery and slips into a pocket.

Most operators master the remote on the first day. For sites that later need scheduled or POS-synced updates, the same PCUs can be driven by a wired terminal or forecourt controller instead — the remote remains a handy on-site backup.

The remote is part of the AVS PCU price-board system — see the full Price Change Unit range. It's covered by AVS's standard 3-year warranty, extendable to 5 years.

Why operators choose it

Economical control, built for the forecourt

A deliberately simple device — everything an attendant needs to change a price, and nothing they don't.

5 simple buttons

A clean five-button layout for selecting the grade and entering the new digits. No menus to learn — staff are confident after one demonstration.

Up to 100 m range

Reliable line-of-sight range of up to 100 metres lets you stand anywhere on the forecourt and still reach the price monolith.

AA-battery powered

Runs on a single, off-the-shelf AA battery. Nothing to wire and nothing to charge — keep a spare in the drawer and you're covered.

Wireless radio link

Communicates with the PCU over a robust wireless radio link suited to outdoor forecourt use. Frequency details available on request.

Pocket-sized

A compact 92 × 40 × 15 mm handheld in a high-grade plastic casing — light enough to live in an attendant's pocket all shift.

Quality tested

Need more than a handheld?

Scaling beyond single-site control

The remote is ideal for standalone stations. For multi-site networks or POS-synced pricing, AVS offers wired and integrated control.

  • For dealer groups or stations that prefer a fixed in-shop unit, the Price Sign Terminal offers a 16-key LCD controller and can drive multiple PCUs.
  • For pump-and-pole price synchronisation from the till, see POS / IFSF integration, which links your PCUs to the forecourt controller over RS232–RS485.
  • All three control methods drive the same AVS Price Change Units — so you can start with the remote and add wired or POS control as your sites grow.
