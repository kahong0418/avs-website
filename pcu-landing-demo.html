The forecourt is a hard place for electronics

Most price displays fail at the one thing they exist to do

Direct sun, driving rain, 12 hours of heat a day and a price that has to change on schedule. A display that washes out, lets water in, or forces a staff member up a ladder is a display that costs you — in fuel volume and in call-outs.

Washed out in daylight

A dim display is invisible at the exact moment a driver decides which station to pull into. Brightness on the forecourt is not a luxury — it is the whole point.

Water gets in

Malaysian rain finds every unsealed seam. Once moisture reaches the boards, you are replacing digits — not cleaning them.

Slow, risky price changes

Sending staff up a monolith with a pole every time head office moves the price is slow, weather-dependent and a safety liability.

The Price Change Unit

One sealed system: display, controller and remote

The PCU is built as a complete unit, not a bag of parts. High-brightness LED digits sit behind a UV-stable face, the whole enclosure is silicone-sealed to IP67, and the controller talks to your POS or accepts a wireless price change from up to 100 m away.

  • Sunlight-legible LED digits, dimmable for night — bright by day, easy on the eye after dark
  • IP67 silicone-sealed enclosure — burn-in tested and vibration tested before it leaves the floor
  • Change the price from the counter in seconds — wireless remote or POS / IFSF controller link
  • Built to standard or to your monolith — custom digit sizes and small quantities welcome
