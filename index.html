Engineered to survive the forecourt

Reliability you can install and forget

Every AVS product is tested before it ships — because a price digit that fails on a pylon is a service truck you can't afford.

  • 48-hour burn-in on every batch
  • Cold-chamber (freezer) test to expose dry joints
  • Vibration testing of connectors, harnesses & solder
  • Conformal-coated PCBs against moisture & corrosion
  • Automated SMT, wave-soldering & Plug-&-Place lines
  • Premium US & German LEDs only — Cree & Osram
