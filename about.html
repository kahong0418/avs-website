About AVS Display

LED forecourt specialists — manufacturing outdoor price displays and lighting in Malaysia for over 9 years.

Who we are

A Malaysian manufacturer built for the forecourt

AVS Display Sdn Bhd (online: AVS Display Malaysia) has designed and manufactured outdoor LED displays and lighting for petrol-station forecourts for over 9 years. What began in Kuala Lumpur is now run from our own facility in Malaysia, where we engineer, assemble and test the hardware that keeps fuel prices visible and forecourts lit, day and night.

Our core line covers LED Price Change Units (PCUs) for monoliths and pylons, LED tubes for signage and forecourt identity lighting, and LED backlight systems such as modules and lens strips for light boxes and monolith faces. Every unit is built to be integration-ready, linking cleanly to forecourt controllers and POS over serial RS232–RS485 using the IFSF protocol so pump and pole prices stay in sync.

We work to one standard: "Leading the LED Revolution for Petrol Stations and Beyond." That means hardware engineered for the outdoors, customised to each operator's site, and supported through the full life of the installation.

How we build

Engineered for the outdoors, tested without shortcuts

Forecourt hardware lives outside — under sun, rain, heat and constant vibration. We build for that environment from the component up, using premium LEDs only and putting every board through a disciplined test regime before it ships.

  • 48-hour burn-in test to catch early-life failures
  • Cold-chamber (freezer) test to expose dry joints
  • Vibration testing for monolith and pylon mounting
  • Conformal-coated PCBs for moisture and dust resistance
  • Automated SMT, wave-soldering and Plug-&-Place production lines
  • Premium Cree and Osram LEDs — no compromise on light source
