Support — Service, Warranty & Spare Parts

We support what we build: from LED Price Change Units to forecourt signage, our own team backs every unit with service, warranty and genuine spare parts.

Lifetime backing

Support for every unit we manufacture

As the manufacturer — not a reseller — AVS keeps the design files, test data and component stock for every Price Change Unit, LED tube, module and signage build we have shipped for over 9 years. That means faster diagnosis, genuine replacement parts and engineers who actually know your installation. Whether you run a single forecourt or a national network across Petronas, Shell, Petron, Vivo or BHP sites, support comes straight from the people who built your displays.

How we help

Support services

Seven ways our team keeps your displays accurate, bright and online.

Technical support

Talk directly to engineers who know the PCU Controller, remote and POS/IFSF link. Email info@avs.my or call 03-8740 9865 for fault diagnosis, photocell and brightness tuning, controller configuration and price-update troubleshooting.

Warranty & service

AVS displays carry a manufacturer warranty of 3 years standard, extendable to 5 — the exact term depends on the market, configuration and service arrangement, and is stated on your quotation. Every unit ships only after a 48-hour burn-in, cold-chamber dry-joint test and vibration test, with conformal-coated PCBs for harsh forecourt conditions. We honour warranty service on covered units and offer planned servicing to keep IP54/IP67 enclosures and connectors weather-tight year-round. We stand behind that commitment: when a board-set replacement was needed outside a supplier's coverage, AVS self-funded the full replacement, around RM100,000, rather than leave the site with a non-working display.

Spare parts & replacements

Genuine replacement digits, Cree/Osram LED panels, PCU Controllers, photocells, 5-button 433 MHz remotes, IP68 connectors and HDPE/aluminium casing parts. Cut-to-fit ATHH Green and ColorLine tube sections, clips and quick-connectors are available to match your original build.

RMA process

Faulty unit? Email info@avs.my with the model, serial and a short fault description (photos help). We issue an RMA reference, confirm whether it is in or out of warranty, and advise return-to-factory or on-site swap. Repaired or replaced parts are retested before they ship back.

Documentation & drawings

Request datasheets, wiring and RS232–RS485 integration diagrams, mechanical drawings, IFSF/CE/RoHS/FCC certificate copies and installation guides for your exact configuration. As manufacturer we hold the records for each project, so documentation matches the units actually on your site.

On-site & project support

For rollouts and complex sites we provide on-site commissioning, controller setup (one PCU Controller drives up to 8 PCUs), POS/IFSF integration assistance and operator training. Multi-site network operators can arrange scheduled maintenance visits across their forecourts.

Export & multi-market support

Beyond Malaysia, AVS displays are exported and supported across Cambodia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Chile and Pakistan. Service and spare parts are handled cross-border for these markets, coordinated directly with our factory.

Support hours

Our service desk is open Mon–Fri 9am–5pm and Sat 9am–1pm. For the fastest response, WhatsApp us, and we reply the same day. Email info@avs.my any time and we will respond on the next working day.
