Edge Lighting Strip

Coloured edge and fascia lighting — a sealed silicone-and-aluminium strip for forecourt canopy edges, fascia lines and signage returns.

Introduction Key Features Key Specification Installation Application Datasheet
Edge & Fascia Lighting

One continuous line of colour along an edge

The AVS edge lighting strip runs a clean, unbroken line of colour along a canopy edge, a fascia, a counter line or a signage return. The LEDs sit in an aluminium profile under a PC cover and are sealed in silicone, so the strip goes outdoors and stays sealed.

The build shown here is the 460~470 nm blue strip supplied for Petron forecourt edge lighting: 1350 mm per piece, 66 LEDs per metre, 12V DC. Runs are made up from these pieces, and there is no minimum order quantity, so a single fascia is as much of a job to us as a rollout.

Installation

Surface mounted, screwed or taped

The strip mounts flat to the surface it lights. Screw it through the profile where the substrate takes a fixing, or bed it on the blue double-sided tape where it cannot be drilled. Aluminium or plastic end caps close both ends, and each piece carries a 200 mm lead-out cable.

Tell us the metres and we will come back with the piece count and the driver the run needs. There is no minimum order quantity, so one fascia is a job we will quote.

Application

Where the edge strip fits

Anywhere a brand needs a clean line of colour that reads at night without lighting the whole surface.

  • Forecourt canopy edges and fascia lines
  • Signage returns and channel-letter edges
  • Building outlines and feature edges
  • Counter and shopfront edge lighting
